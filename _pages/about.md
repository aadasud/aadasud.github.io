I'm a graduate student in Aerospace Engineering at Texas A&M University, 
pursuing a Master of Engineering with a focus on Guidance, Navigation, and 
Control (GNC). My interests center on optimal control, celestial mechanics, 
and state estimation for spacecraft trajectory and mission design.

## Research & Current Work

I'm currently a Research Assistant at the Texas A&M Bioastronautics & Human 
Performance Laboratory, where I develop real-time biometrics processing 
tools and predictive models for Orthostatic Intolerance event detection 
and prevention.

## Background

I completed my B.S. in Aerospace Engineering at Texas A&M in 2025, where I 
served as Lead Structures Engineer and GNC Engineer for my senior capstone 
team, redesigning the Apollo Lunar Module for a modern Artemis-era mission 
profile, and as Structures Product Owner for the TAMU SAE Aero Design Team.

This site showcases a selection of my coursework and independent projects 
in GNC and orbital mechanics, spanning state estimation, optimal control, 
orbital transfer design, and low-thrust trajectory optimization.
